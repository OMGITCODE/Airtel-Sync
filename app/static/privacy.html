ONEJOURNEY · LOCAL DEMONSTRATION

Privacy, clearly explained.

OneJourney demonstrates small-business onboarding with fictional challenge records. Use only the supplied specimen documents. Do not upload real identity documents or customer details.

Document upload requires your consent. OCR runs locally on this computer and compares values with mock registries; no government registry, payment provider, or live Airtel system is contacted.

What is processed

  • The local Ollama advisor receives chat after common emails, Indian phone numbers, GSTINs, and Udyam IDs are redacted.
  • Business profile facts remain suggestions until you confirm them. The advisor does not receive KYC fields.
  • OCR extracts document type, business names, dates, and registration identifiers. Preview text masks common identifiers; identifier fields are masked unless you choose to reveal them.
  • The supplied README describes all challenge records, registries, and specimen images as fictional. Funnel responses contain aggregates, not individual lead records.

Where it is stored and for how long

Saved journeys, entered registry identifiers, consent, OCR results, and simulated order events stay in the local SQLite database selected by ONEJOURNEY_DB. The default file is onejourney.sqlite3. They remain until you delete that database; there is no automatic expiry. The database is not encrypted at rest.

To erase saved prototype records, stop the app and delete the configured SQLite database. This does not remove the challenge files in app/data/.

Prototype limits

This project is not a production verification or ordering service. Registry, eligibility, pricing, and activation are simulations. It has no production authentication or access control. Keep it on a trusted local device and do not expose it to the public internet. Check competition terms before redistributing the supplied data outside this repository.
