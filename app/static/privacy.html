ONEJOURNEY · LOCAL DEMONSTRATION · 7 NOVEMBER 2026

Privacy, clearly explained.

Use only the supplied synthetic challenge records. Do not upload real identity documents or customer information. OneJourney is not an Airtel production service and has no production authentication.

KYC upload requires purpose-specific consent. Images are processed in memory by local OpenCV/Tesseract OCR and are not saved. Extracted fields, masked previews, identifiers, consent, profile, quotes, chat history, handoffs and simulated order events are stored in the local SQLite database, which is not encrypted at rest.

Local processing

Only advisor chat goes to the configured local Ollama model; common email, phone, GSTIN and Udyam patterns are redacted first. KYC fields never go to the model. The guided questionnaire and catalogue recommender work without Ollama. WhatsApp preview is simulated and uses the same local advisor route. No government, payment, Airtel or other hosted service is contacted.

Retention and deletion

Journey-linked records expire 7 days after journey creation. The app removes expired journeys at startup and checks hourly while running. Use Delete my data in the app to erase the active journey’s profile, extracted KYC results, consent, quotes, chat, handoffs and simulated order history immediately. The supplied synthetic challenge dataset in app/data/ is not deleted.

Prototype limits

Registry, pricing, eligibility and activation are simulations. Keep the app bound to localhost; do not expose it to the public internet. The supplied data pack describes its sample businesses and identifiers as fictional. The GitHub repository remains private because the competition materials do not grant public redistribution rights. Review competition terms before sharing the supplied data elsewhere.
